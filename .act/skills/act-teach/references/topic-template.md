# Topic file template

`topic.md` sits at `.act-local/learn/<topic>/topic.md`, in `language-chat`. Short, rewritten in place.

```markdown
# <Topic>

## Goal
<1-3 lines: why the human learns this, how they will notice they can do it, what is out of scope>

## Sources
- <url or path> - <what it is for>
- <url or path> - <what it is for>

## Learning record
### Prior knowledge
- <what the human said they already know>

### Learned (with evidence)
- <point> - <lesson number and the passed check or correct answer>

### Corrected misconceptions
- <what was believed> -> <what is right> - <lesson number>

## Preferences
- <pace, depth, language of examples, ...>
```

Only evidenced points go under "Learned". A point the human merely read does not.
